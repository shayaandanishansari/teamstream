TeamStream

Not a ticket system. A live map of where the mission's effort is right now — built for teams with no manager, only brothers.
any button, pressed by anyone — hot = live now, warm = recently touched

What it optimizes for

Mission completion. Not individual accountability. It doesn't matter who finishes it.

The core unit

The task is the durable object. People flow through it — ownership is fluid, not assigned.

How it actually works

01

Anyone can press any task. No claiming, no "assign to me" step, no permission gate. Open access is the point.

02

Pressed = highlighted. Live at a glance across the whole board. Hover reveals who's on it, since when, and their last note — detail on demand, not by default.

03

Multiple people on one task is convergence, not conflict. Two brothers on the same wall means it breaks faster — the board shows it, never blocks it.

04

Spaces roll every sprint. A live snapshot of current mission state — not the permanent record.

05

The archive underneath never rotates. Debug breakthroughs and wall-breakers found in month 3 stay searchable in year 2, long after the sprint-space that produced them is gone.

The dashboard question

Not "who did what." Where is our total effort concentrated, where's it thin, where's nothing happening despite being critical — resource allocation across the whole team, including yourself. A campaign map, not a leaderboard.

Who it's for

Small, high-trust, years-long-mission teams with no external manager imposing structure. Always a strongest link and a weakest one — both still brothers. Visibility exists to close ranks around whoever's stuck, never to rank or flag anyone.
